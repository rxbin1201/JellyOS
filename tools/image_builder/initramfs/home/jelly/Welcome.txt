Welcome to the JellyOS desktop!

This is your home folder (/home/jelly). Everything here belongs to you.

- The JellyOS button in the taskbar opens the launcher.
- Files lets you browse, copy and delete files, also on the FAT volumes
  under /volumes.
- Settings changes the theme, the text size and the keyboard layout.
- Terminal gives you the shell.

The default account is "jelly" with the password "jelly".
